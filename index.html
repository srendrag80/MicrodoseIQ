🔬 Under the Hood: Receptor Pharmacology, Strain Variance & Chitin Guide ▼

Why Weight Scaling is Bounded: Clinical trials (Johns Hopkins & University of Basel) demonstrate that classic serotonergic psychedelics act primarily on central 5-HT2A receptors in the cortex, which do not scale linearly with adipose tissue. However, peripheral metabolic clearance and distribution volume vary slightly with body mass. MicrodoseIQ applies a conservative, bounded physiological adjustment while preventing high-weight users from accidentally crossing the perceptual threshold.

Natural Biomass & Strain Note (Cubensis vs. PE / Wood-Lovers): Cultivated strains like Penis Envy (PE), Albino Penis Envy (APE), or wild wood-lovers (P. azurescens / cyanescens) frequently contain 1.5x to 2.5x higher alkaloid concentrations than standard Golden Teacher or B+, and single mushrooms within the same flush can vary significantly. Always homogenize (grind and mix) dried biomass and test a new batch at the Gentle Starting Floor.

Enteric Comfort & Chitin Extraction: Whole mushroom cell walls are composed of indigestible fungal chitin, which irritates gut lining and stimulates enteric 5-HT3 receptors (nausea/cramping). Steep ground mushroom material in ~80°C hot water for 15 minutes with fresh ginger or peppermint, then strain out the solids for clean sub-perceptual absorption (avoid Lemon Tek for microdosing, as citric acid accelerates peak onset).

⚠️ Never Use Tap or Spring Water

Municipal tap water contains residual chlorine and chloramines that break the fragile ergoline ring and destroy LSD on contact. Use 100% distilled water plus 20% high-proof vodka/ethanol to prevent bacterial or mold growth over months.

💎 Dissolving Pyramid Gel Tabs

Gel tabs suspend LSD in gelatin. Never slice them dry (shards shatter unpredictably). Warm your measured distilled water slightly (body temperature ~37°C, never boiling), drop the gel tab in, and shake gently until the gelatin matrix dissolves completely.

Morris A+B: Turns bright lavender/purple for Ketamine HCl (blue/green indicates novel cathinones or PCE/PCP analogs).

🎗️ Fentanyl Test Strip Dilution Ratios (DanceSafe Protocol):

To prevent false positives on fentanyl strips while reliably detecting fentanyl analogs: dissolve 10 mg of powder in 5 mL (1 teaspoon) of water when testing MDMA, 2C-B, or amphetamines; dissolve 10 mg in 1 mL of water when testing Ketamine or Cocaine. Dip strip to the wavy line for 15 seconds and wait 3 minutes on a flat surface (1 red line = Positive / Danger; 2 red lines = Negative, even if the second line is very faint).
